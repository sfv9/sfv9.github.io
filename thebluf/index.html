The BLUFby Shannon Vaughn

As Project Gatekeeper goes live, Army plans integration with frontier AI models

DefenseScoop · Oct 28, 2026
BLUF: The Army switched on Project Gatekeeper, an effort to replace the months-long manual ATO process with automated checks on vendor code.
  • Vendors share code with the Army's DevSecOps platform and get validated in near real time.
  • The stated goal: a company commits code and gets a pass from the pipeline within minutes.
For new entrants: Security approval is often the longest step for a new software vendor. An automated ATO path can shorten the time from award to actual use.
My Take: This is the most useful thing the Army has done for software startups this year, because the ATO is where good pilots go to die.
Why read it

News is everywhere. What it means for you isn't.

  1. It's filtered for one reader.

    Companies with commercial products trying to win federal work, not the primes and not policy wonks.

  2. Every story ends in a so-what.

    The BLUF tells you what happened. For new entrants and My Take tell you what to do about it.

  3. It's timed to the money.

    Each issue places the week on the federal fiscal calendar, so you know whether to push, wait or get ready.

  4. It's sourced.

    Every summary links to the reporting or official source it came from. No made-up numbers.
