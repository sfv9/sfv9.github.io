The Federal Year

Federal money runs on a clock. Most founders can't see it.

Congress, agency contracting offices and the Pentagon's planners each run their own calendar. Your deal moves when all three line up. This page shows where we are on that clock today, what each month is for, and when to start if you need a contract by a certain date.

The Federal Money Wheel

Twelve months, three calendars, one wheel

The year starts October 1. The outer ring is Congress (where the money comes from), the middle ring is buyers (how much contracting offices are spending), and the inner ring is DoD planning (the budget two years out). Tap a month to see what it's for.
